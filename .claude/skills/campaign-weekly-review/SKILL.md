# Weekly review

Read `AGENTS.md`, `workspace/plan.md` and the whole workbook (`show Overview`, then
each sheet for the last seven days of `Event History`).

1. **What happened this week**, from `Event History` only: applications, interviews,
   offers, rejections, messages sent, replies, posts and their numbers. Counts and the
   names that matter.
2. **Against the plan.** Compare with the four-week targets in `plan.md`. Say plainly
   where they are behind and the likely reason, from the data, not a guess.
3. **Stale rows.** Leads older than 10 days still `Lead saved - not applied`,
   applications with no update in 14 days, follow-ups overdue, `Ready` posts not
   posted, interviews `Done` with no outcome after 7 days. Suggest the action for each (apply, close, nudge, drop).
4. **Review sheet.** List open rows; resolve any the week's evidence settles, with the
   evidence, in one write.
5. **Content** (if on): which posts did best and worst on 7-day numbers, using li-audit's
   method. Missing numbers: ask them to read them off LinkedIn; never estimate.
6. **Next week.** Three changes at most, each with why. Get a yes before editing
   `plan.md` or `config.json`.

Record nothing as an outcome in this routine unless the evidence is already in hand.
