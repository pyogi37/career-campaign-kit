# Outreach

Needs the `outreach` module. Read `AGENTS.md` and `workspace/voice.md` first.

## Picking people

1. Start from the goal. For a job search: the hiring manager or team lead for roles in
   `Applications` at `Ready to apply` or `Applied`, then recruiters, then alumni or
   past colleagues at target companies. For clients: the buyer, then the person who
   feels the problem. Respect their weekly number and the `Off limits` rows.
2. For each person, find one real, specific reason to write, from their public posts,
   their company's news or the role. No reason, no message.
3. Check `Outreach` and `Event History` first: never a second first-message to someone
   already contacted, and never a new invite while one is pending.

## Drafting

Use li-dm for the invite note, first message and two follow-ups. Each passes the four
writing checks. Record each person as an `Outreach` row at `Drafted`, with the drafts,
`Why them`, and `Linked record` (the JOB ID if there is one).

## Sending and replies

- Show each message with the person's name and profile link. They send it, or say yes
  to that one message for you to send. On a yes for the batch as shown, send exactly
  that batch and nothing else.
- After it is sent: an `Event History` row (`Invite sent` or `Message sent`, with the
  text sent) and the `Outreach` update to that stage with `Last sent` and
  `Next follow-up` (follow-up 1 after about 5 working days, follow-up 2 a week after
  that, then stop).
- A reply: record it as an event (`Replied`, quote the key line), move the stage, and
  draft the answer. Close the loop rather than asking another question.

## "Follow-ups due"

`show Outreach` and list rows whose `Next follow-up` is today or earlier, with the
drafted follow-up and anything new about that person. Same sending rule as above.
